## Design and conditional results

The calendar is **2020-01-01 inclusive to 2025-01-01 exclusive: 1,827 days and 1,305 Monday–Friday working days**, without added holidays. Both arms start from the same 30-person state, seed 42 and world index 0. Complete initial agents, logical ledger and header were compared, allowing only the predeclared treatment configuration difference. The treatments are hierarchy with no adoption, and sublinear DAMS with parent-guild adoption on day zero.

DAMS produced less work and ended with less cash, while decision regret was slightly lower in this pair. These opposing outcomes are retained. There are no confidence intervals, significance claims or formal precision conclusions from this single engineering pair. ## Mechanism and validation boundaries

r7 adds opt-in reporting, collaboration and verification networks, prior-day task dependencies, and competing production, quality and learning objectives with causal time/capacity effects. The two task departments are distinct from the three final installed enterprise departments. Role exclusions alone do not establish independent reviewer motives or resistance to political capture. Neutral ID-based departments and the bounded task chain remain synthetic assumptions rather than calibrated organizational networks or a general project graph.

Population growth from 30 to 34 is the transfer of four acquired employees, not evidence of DAMS-induced organic hiring. Installed capacity expansion is paid and operating-state-driven, but vacancy-fill, exit and leave probabilities in this fixture remain zero. The inherited M&A mechanism carries a finite target-history capsule, personnel, assets, liabilities, knowledge and unresolved evidence, with transaction/integration costs. Under `preserve-autonomy`, the acquired guild retains its original hierarchy/central policy in both arms. This is not company-wide forced DAMS conversion, two independently evolving complete enterprises, or a calibrated M&A and cultural-integration study.

The organization accountant independently reconstructs role, time, task, potential, learning and fatigue accounting without calling the producer's topology, preparation, allocation, finish or reviewer transitions. It shares journal parsing, the calendar and keyed RNG. Some inherited enterprise validators share producer transition functions. These program checks and exact checkpoint replay are separate from complete independent economic/model validation, parameter recovery against empirical truth, calibration and unseen-enterprise validation.
