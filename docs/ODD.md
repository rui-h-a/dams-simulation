# DAMS-ABM 0.1.0 executable specification

## Purpose, evidence and DAMS connection

Purpose is theoretical exploration and conditional mechanism assessment of DAMS, not empirical prediction. RQ2 evaluates DP-Continuity, domain-local DP-Modularity and the capacity cost of DP-Verifiability/DP-HumanOverride. RQ3 compares explicit trust assumptions in the evidence plane. Mathematical allocation checks address formal authority; organization runs add feedback and fallible observations. The model has not been calibrated to a firm, a survey or a human-behaviour dataset. No numerical coefficient is derived from a psychological theory. All supported numerical parameters are design choices or stress assumptions.

Entities are synthetic people, one personal account per person, teams, one guild/domain per person, sites and one organization. The external environment generates proposals, task difficulty and site shocks. Person i belongs to guild i mod G, team floor(floor(i/G)/team_size) within that guild, and site guild mod S. The site mapping is a design assumption and can leave configured sites unoccupied when S>G; sites are not blockchain validators. Team and guild membership are deterministic, while individual latent skill, observed tenure, care constraint, authority sensitivity and reciprocity are independently generated. This is a controlled heterogeneous population, **not** a data-fitted synthetic population or justified cross-demographic joint distribution.

The model uses a team collaboration pool plus one directed ring edge i→(i+1) mod N. Formal rank scores have a separate role in weighting. Distinct reporting, knowledge, validation, social and faction graph layers are not individually estimated. Department/project records, multiple guild membership, external customer agents, protected demographics, changing employment and exits are outside this version. Their factor disposition is explicit in factor_disposition.json. Team-help production is a stylized complementarity term, not a uniquely identified decomposition of joint marginal products.

## State, units and information

Agent fields are id, guild, team, site; latent skill; observed tenure score; care time; authority-response sensitivity; reciprocity; fatigue F∈[0,1]; experienced-record trust T∈[0,1]; learning L∈[0,2]; confirmed domain credit C≥0; formal share a∈[0,1]; and previous effort for reinforcement. `autonomy` in code is a synthetic response-sensitivity coefficient, not a measured psychological-autonomy construct. `autonomy_response` names a conjectured response to share relative to equal eligibility; zero and reversed responses are legal controls.

One day is one hour-equivalent work budget, a model scaling unit rather than an asserted workweek. Care removes U(0,0.3) of that daily budget. A review record takes 0.1 human hour-equivalents, an appeal 0.15, participation reserves 0.03 and learning 0.02. Cooperative help reserves 0.05×cooperation. Review/appeal reservations are uniform divisible pool allocations, expiring when unused. They reduce production opportunity; the current version does not model integer reviewer identities or minute-by-minute individual scheduling. Backends consume extra *verification resource units* and therefore fewer records fit the common processing budget. These units are not automatically human hours, currency or measured CPU time.

Agents know their own previous recognition, learning, fatigue/trust and current formal share; they receive noisy proposal signals. Latent skill is used only by the environment to generate work. Proposal value θ is researcher-only evaluation truth, never an argument to the voting choice except through the signal-generation observation channel. Reviewers receive observed credit and an audit alarm. `fraudulent` is an evaluator flag used to construct sensor error and posthoc detection counts; acceptance reads `audit_detected`, not that flag or latent output. Appealing members use observed credit and experienced trust. No future confirmed credit affects an earlier vote.

## Initialization and counter random streams

Initial skill s=exp(0.4 Z), Z~N(0,1). Tenure, authority sensitivity and reciprocity are U(0,1); care=0.3U. F=0.1, T=0.7, L=C=0. Morning shares use explicit equal fallback when all eligible C are zero. Every draw is keyed by seed, independent-world ID, process, day/entity/event ID and SHA-256; a 53-bit uniform is mapped through Box–Muller for normals. Policy/backend are excluded from exogenous keys. Branching, agent visitation order and checkpoint/restart do not advance a mutable cursor. Network/population scenarios may alter which variables a draw influences, but do not add independent samples to the same world.

## Schedule and exact submodels

`Model.step` has the following sequence:

1. Decay prior confirmed credit by 1−δ. Commit previously accepted records whose ready day has arrived. Credit created on day c and committed on day t is q(1−δ)^(t−c); duplicate event IDs never score twice. A configured consensus-quorum outage holds commits and reports unavailable days. It does not simulate a BFT protocol.
2. On t mod update_interval=0, recalculate guild formal shares, then freeze a,T,F,L for the day. Equal shares use α=0 as an explicit eligibility rule; linear uses α=1; sublinear uses 0<α<1. Performance hierarchy sorts confirmed C; tenure hierarchy sorts observed fixed appointment scores. Both use configurable equal-sized rank tiers and average positional weights across exact ties. Cap water filling and split-gain functions are separately tested analytical mechanisms, not enabled baseline policies.
3. For each guild, draw θ~N(0,1). Agent signal is θ+σ_decision Z_i/(1+L_i)+σ_shared Z_g. Participants are drawn with probability clip(0.25+0.65T−0.35F). Binary support is signal≥0; participating formal shares are renormalized. A weighted vote≥0.5 adopts the proposal. With no participants the decision is unresolved. Ex-post regret is |θ| when the binary action is wrong, and |θ| for an unresolved action. This is a clairvoyant feasible-action evaluation bound, **not** an attainable realistic managerial comparator. The binary action is scored within the day; execution queues, deliberation and decision latency are not calibrated or modeled.
4. Let h=clip(reciprocity×T×(1−F)). Remaining production time is max(0,1−care−0.1×review_capacity−0.15×appeal_capacity−0.03−0.02−0.05h). Baseline desired effort is 0.5+0.35T+sensitivity×autonomy_response×(guild_size×a−1)−0.4F−care, clipped to remaining time. Satisficing instead chooses 0.45 after C/max(1,t)≥0.5, otherwise 0.8, then adds the conjectured share response and subtracts 0.3F. Reinforcement uses previous effort+0.1(T−0.5)+the share response−0.2F. These are structural alternatives, not fitted rational-choice rules.
5. Compute team mean help from simultaneous choices and average it with the ring neighbour's help. Task difficulty D=0.5+U; multiplicative quality noise=exp(0.1Z). Environmental site-0 fault multiplies output by 0.7 during the configured window. Individual generated work is s(1+L)e(1−F)exp(0.1Z)×site_factor/D×(1+cooperation_strength×help). Sum is work units; cooperation is separate and is not an observed organizational-profit measure.
6. Observed credit q=max(0,work+σ_review Z). Submit event `work:day:person`, ready next day. A forged claim increases q by equal per-attacker budget while retaining evaluator fraud truth. Audit alarms occur with probability 0.6 for forged and 0.02 for honest claims; these probabilities are explicit design assumptions. Source truth stays fallible across all evidence backends. Duplicate attacks resubmit the same event. Free-riding additionally reduces real effort and cooperation. Censorship drops submissions with probability min(1,budget/N)×configured backend exposure for targets outside the attacker set. Attackers are the first min(N,ceil(budget/0.1)) stable person IDs. Each attacker reserves budget/attacker_count hour-equivalents before choosing production; all four attacks consume exactly the same configured time, enter fatigue workload, and satisfy the daily individual bound. An infeasible budget fails rather than silently shrinking. This is an explicit model-time opportunity cost, not a measured financial/adversarial expense. Some reserved time can replace otherwise idle time; productive opportunity cost can differ endogenously by policy.
7. Review prior-day submissions in (ready day,event ID) heap order. Daily pooled reviewer credits are guild_size×review_capacity; divide by backend resource multiplier (defaults 1,1.2,1.5). Unused whole capacity expires; fractional remainder carries. Accept q≥0.25 with no audit alarm, then schedule settlement after configurable backend delays (defaults 1,2,3 days). Reject otherwise. Rejection changes T by −0.025; successful commitment by +0.015. Trust changes follow actual events, not regime labels. A claim with q≥0.25 and T>0.2 can appeal after the configured delay. Appeal capacity is separate; reinspection flags fraud with probability 0.9 and honest claims with probability 0.01. Successful appeals re-admit credit. There is no retroactive revocation of already committed credit in this version.
8. Update fatigue F'=clip(F+0.15e−0.1(1−e)−0.03), L'=clip(L+learning_rate×e,0,2), and previous effort. Freeze-versus-update separation prevents entity order from changing simultaneous help/output. Capture snapshots at trace cadence and retain explicit unfinished record/appeal counts at the finite horizon. No automatic draining or hidden extension of the horizon occurs.

## Trust scenarios and experiment controls

Backend cost, settlement delay and administrative censorship exposure are three independently configurable vectors in central/witness/consensus order. Defaults are an illustrative conditional ladder, not a security finding: the zero consensus exposure presumes an honest live quorum, and the witness midpoint is a stress assumption. Set all exposures/costs/delays equal to isolate policy; vary them or use a quorum-unavailable period to test failures and adverse conditions. Centralization may be adequate when operators are trusted. No number of node containers, actual independent controllers, key-management process, geographic latency, storage permanence, secrecy, cryptographic attestation or BFT safety/liveness is established by these three labels. Solidity/EVM tests live separately in contracts/ and do not turn this simulator into a distributed ledger.

Shape, update interval, decay, observation error, decision/shared signal error, help complementarity, authority response, behavioural structure, review/appeal capacity and backend assumptions are separate controls. `reproduce` performs five policies×three backends for a fixed number of independent worlds, preserving raw rows. Dynamic attack outcomes must be compared with the same policy/backend/world without attacks. Analytical identity-splitting uses its own within-policy unsplit baseline; dynamic unique-person eligibility does not test identity admission. No universal winner is built into the evidence claim.

## Measures and validation

Authority/linear-credit TV is ½Σ|a−b|, and signed gap is G(a)−G(C). Mean absolute deviation would be 2TV/N and is never called time lag. Participating-weight TV records divergence from frozen formal shares; it is not an estimate of real authority through information/persuasion. Confirmation and appeal times are days for completed records; unfinished counts accompany their conditional summaries. Backlog excludes appeals and includes unreviewed and accepted-but-unsettled records. Review, appeal, effort, verification units, output, regret, participation and runtime are separate units, never collapsed to uncalibrated money or welfare.

Tests include independent direct-power and pairwise-Gini oracles; finite/extreme/empty/all-zero/tie/cap cases; counter-stream invariance; no early credit; real behavioural effect of free-riding; equal attack budgets; duplicate-credit suppression; queue pressure; source-compatible checkpoint/restart and forward/reversed visitation equivalence for policy/backend cells; import side-effect freedom; output hashes; alternative behaviour and quorum-outage recovery. Restart is engineering recovery, not statistical parameter/model recovery or empirical validation. No independently implemented full ABM or optimized differential implementation currently exists. Future pattern calibration and holdout checks remain separate research work.

Baseline time is approximately O(days×(N+claim_heap_ops+guild_sort)), with O(N+unfinished claims+retained delay samples+trace snapshots) space. Every person emits a claim per day; storing seen IDs and completed delay samples grows with event count. The input event-count bound rejects N×days beyond max_events. RSS is sampled every 1,000 agents during initialization, between days and after final-state output; exceeding max_rss_mb fails the run. This is sampled process-high-water enforcement, not an OS guarantee that intra-day transient RSS never overshoots the threshold. Platforms without RSS enforcement fail. RSS failures do not allocate a fresh large checkpoint, and therefore may not be restartable; any previously saved checkpoint remains intact. Explicit checkpoint-day and wall-time failure are tested restart paths. This readable implementation does not imply safe execution at million-agent scales. Source and run manifests determine the exact implementation underlying any result.
