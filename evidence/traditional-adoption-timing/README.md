# Adoption timing in the traditional-firm scenario

The 65 retained worlds start with 300 members and share two years of prehistory before the five-year policy window. The timing study assigns a retained hierarchy, staged DAMS on an independently witnessed record, and backlog-triggered DAMS on that same procedure. Immediate DAMS with fixed-day adoption is from the earlier traditional-firm comparison.

The retained daily files and summaries of 325 arms were checked against their arm receipts. In each world, the triggered and fixed-day DAMS daily files are byte-identical. The hierarchy daily files from the two releases are also byte-identical. The two releases retain distinct source and execution identities. The shared digests record daily-file equality, and the packet provides the numerical inputs for the two direct comparisons.

Run `python3 -I -S -B verify.py`. The program reports first adoption days, then the staged-minus-triggered difference in the net operating result and mean confirmation delay. The operating result uses synthetic resource units per member-year. Delay in days is conditional on confirmation. The two 95% Student-t intervals are exploratory and unadjusted. This packet recomputes retained outcomes. It does not execute the model or independently reconstruct the event journals.
