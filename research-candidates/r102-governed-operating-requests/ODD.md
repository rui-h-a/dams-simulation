calendar, operations, growth, task structure and two-firm ownership mechanisms.

`longitudinal-R102-governed-operating-requests-candidate-1`; the organization
subsystem remains its inherited R10 schema2. The joint ownership wrapper retains
its inherited version. None of these identifiers upgrades calibration/OOS.

`governed_operating_requests` declares schema1 and one original charter guild.

work. This is a prior charter, not a vote on an ungenerated proposal. After actual
evening operations/cash/cost settlement and growth.observe, the unchanged pinned
helper quotes an exact original hiring/infrastructure request for the next day.
The proposal's observed inputs, scope, cost, reserve, quantity and delay are
journaled before votes. Original observed margin, original noise/learning and
trust/fatigue participation rules, morning shares and .5 weighted majority
remain explicit synthetic assumptions. No favorable coefficient is added.

weight0. Participation requires the earlier funded reservation. The persisted
full ballot must match actual individual vote rows and cost settlement. Used
reservation is charged once; unused reservation is released after production
as idle opportunity capacity. It creates no output and no free second vote.

regret metrics retain their domain scope and cannot become operating evidence.

Next morning the unchanged original growth backend consumes the exact accepted
request with actual cash/active/payroll/operating/expansion context. Rejected,
unresolved, absent, failed, stale and expired requests cannot cause unvoted new
investment. Existing installation/hiring completion, contraction/cancellation
obligations continue according to the original backend. Quoted reserve, original
setup costs and delays are rechecked, not shortened by governance acceptance.

consumption-context journal record within the existing daily SAVEPOINT. It
contains complete actual configuration and boundary inputs. Full Model semantic
validation receives actual Model configuration, re-quotes proposals and replays
every transition through the pinned original backend. Before/after/events/cash/
payment/status must match exactly; nonfunded cannot invent stocks or completion.

charter costs, votes and cumulative governed metrics. Same-boundary replay
returns paid_now0 and cannot emit a duplicate context/receipt. Daily rollback
uses the original durable-checkpoint recovery discipline. No new ledger table
or database is added; the source/version and bounded cache schema are distinct.

not independent full economic validation. Charter delegation, behavioral
