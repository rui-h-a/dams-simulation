# Conditional acquisition engineering candidate

This optional model is `longitudinal-enterprise-ma-candidate-2`. It is derived from the preserved causal-r3 runtime and is separate from every running or completed scientific study. Passing these controls does not admit a new formal study, validate a real company, or make prior candidate results interchangeable. No governance coefficient is selected to make DAMS win. An internal guild merger is not a business acquisition.

## States that have causal effects

A declared target has a distinct seller, bounded availability, named employees with existing birth/skill/learning/trust/fatigue states, installed seats/guilds/departments/sites, treasury, financial debt, consideration, prior governance/backend, organizational knowledge, and an immutable history/evidence capsule. Parent and target source identities remain linked after integration. Original target evidence retains its amount, unit, quality and event identity. Pending contribution claims remain pending and then enter a paid quarantine review; they never turn directly into parent authority. These are declared synthetic initial states, not observed corporate records or calibrated M&A targets.

Closing changes ownership and financial stocks. It does not immediately add usable agents, productive seats or parent authority. Target treasury is transferred exactly once; seller consideration is recorded separately. Acquired financial debt and acquisition loans affect actual debt interest and cash. Paid integration progresses on workdays. Following the final paid integration workday, a later calendar boundary can activate retained employees and installed assets. Integration failures preserve owned idle assets, original evidence and a loss history. Individual integration departures remove that employee from the transferable inventory without erasing source identity or evidence. After transfer, ordinary longitudinal retirement/exit rules still act on the acquired employees; the acquisition map records their historical transfer, while the parent lifecycle record determines current employment.

## Decision and accounting equations

For target employee count N0, prior realized revenue and cost signals R and C, current parent workforce target H, cash K, consideration P, transaction cost T, integration duration q, systems cost a and payroll b, the reserved exposure is

    reserve = max(C, H) * reserve_workdays + N0 * q * (a + b)
    past_margin = (R - C) / max(R, C, 1e-12)
    loan = max(0, P + T + reserve - K)

A target is purchased only with prior observed workdays, an eligible past margin, optional observed order backlog, no conflicting pending construction/hiring, sufficient installed/identity capacity, a declared lender limit, and consolidated closing debt within the declared limit. Target cash cannot finance its own purchase before ownership. The controller does not read latent proposal values, future outcomes, policy rankings or validation data. Only one target is being integrated at a time and a transfer day cannot also close another target. Resource ceilings are explicit engineering bounds and are separate from initial population.

At closing:

    parent_cash_delta = loan + target_cash - P - T
    target_cash_delta = -target_cash
    seller_cash_delta = P
    lender_cash_delta = -loan
    service_provider_cash_delta = T
    parent_financial_debt_delta = loan + target_financial_debt

For each funded integration workday, systems work still costs N0*a even when target employees depart; retained employees additionally cost Nretained*b. Unfunded or nonoperating workdays do not buy integration progress. Repeated unfunded days can fail integration. Integrated target order flow adds to the actual market order arrival equation only after readiness, and installed assets incur ordinary maintenance and alter production capacity and organizational coordination. Acquisition cash receipts and financing are distinct from sales revenue. Transaction, integration and evidence-review costs enter the existing realized growth cost signal, including its non-workday carry logic.

Preserved autonomy retains the target's declared prior governance/backend for new guilds and its organizational knowledge. Centralization applies parent adoption rules and a declared knowledge/learning retention fraction. Both modes consume explicit, different coordination time. This is a testable synthetic coordination/autonomy tradeoff, not a coefficient estimated from the cited studies. It does not infer ability, honesty, culture or national character from demographic labels.

## Theory and evidence boundaries

Puranam, Singh and Zollo (2006), Academy of Management Journal 49(2), 263–280, DOI [10.5465/amj.2006.20786062](https://journals.aom.org/doi/10.5465/amj.2006.20786062), supports conditional coordination/autonomy tradeoffs in technology acquisitions. Only the publisher abstract was available and read here. It does not supply this candidate's coefficients or validate general M&A outcomes.

Stahl and Voigt (2008), Organization Science 19(1), 160–176, DOI [10.1287/orsc.1070.0270](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.1070.0270), supports heterogeneous cultural mechanisms and moderators rather than a universal adverse culture effect. Only the publisher abstract was read here. A cultural-distance model has not been implemented or estimated in this candidate.

The official [IFRS 3 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-3-business-combinations/) supports separating consideration, identifiable acquired assets and assumed liabilities. This model uses synthetic resource units; it does not implement fair-value measurement, goodwill, tax, consolidation reporting or claim IFRS compliance.

## Actual verification and remaining gaps

Tests compare the complete opt-out engine, each daily header, all agents and all ledger tables against an exact, finite causal-r3 source fixture. Other controls cover paid delay, weekend boundaries, all target departures, finite cash/debt limits, acquired order flow and productivity, autonomy/centralization, immutable source evidence, coherent header forgeries, complete raw daily/summary/snapshot validation and exact checkpoint resume.

The extended raw verifier replays declared M&A transitions and does not count as an independent second M&A implementation. The complementary `research_tools.validate_acquisition_accounts` reconstructs source contract prices, loans, target/seller cash, provider receipts, paid integration days, employee identities, idle failure assets and evidence retention directly from the original journal. It does not call Model, an acquisition transition or a growth controller. Deliberately changed integration prices are rejected. This is independent finite accounting validation, not independent scientific validation of the whole ABM.

The target capsule is not a second fully simulated firm. Its full operating history, existing orders/receivables, nonfinancial liabilities, autonomous investment decisions, negotiations, valuations, legal approvals, synergies, competing bidders, supplier obligations, multinational consolidation and subsequent divestiture have not been modeled. A future-orders input is a conditional declared contract flow, not a reconstructed inherited commercial order book. Parent history and target history/evidence are preserved separately; incompatible historical scores are not aggregated. Data calibration, out-of-sample corporate prediction and empirical cultural validation remain incomplete. The three separately reported causal-r3 operational semantic counterexamples are inherited here and have not been silently changed; they require a separate reviewed integration version before formal admission.

## r5 correction boundaries

D1: The original installed-stock recovery constraints are unchanged and now also govern proposed acquisitions before payment and again before activating assets. Department limits depend on the actual proposed guild count, not hypothetical maximum population. A target structure that cannot be represented under this contract is explicitly refused with its unchanged source inventory and zero ownership/payment effects. No acquired asset count is clamped or deleted to pass recovery. This is a limitation of the current representation, not a claim that real firms cannot have redundant departments.

D2: Independent financial reconciliation follows the original journal sequence across targets. An earlier same-day evidence-review fee or integration payment reduces cash available to the next purchase. Lender advances and consolidated closing debt are independently recomputed from source contract terms and the cash/debt at that event, without calling the producer transition. The source-frozen original 11-day D2 evidence is retained as an exact fixture for pure financial readback, never restored as a Model under the new source. A new complete 30-day control uses a two-guild second target so it also obeys D1; all eighth-day price, cash, debt, employee-count and review-cost terms are unchanged. Neither trajectory is a formal sample.

Knowledge scope: The inherited mechanism applies the declared organizational-knowledge value as the same per-guild memory intensity to each acquired guild, including when no target employee remains. For this derivative this is explicitly an uncalibrated assumption about replicable persistent organizational artifacts. It is not an extensive total knowledge budget, cannot be summed across guilds to claim conserved knowledge, and does not identify tacit versus codified knowledge or prove knowledge retention after departures. The original r4 ambiguity and reviewer observations remain preserved. This limited correction does not alter that mechanism or turn it into empirical evidence.
